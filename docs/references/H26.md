# On odd covering systems with few prime factors

*Summary [H26] · I. Hackmon, Zenodo preprint, 10 Apr 2026, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.19495464. Not peer-reviewed.*

*Summarized by: Claude Fable 5.1 for Ryan Wu.*

**TL;DR.** No odd distinct covering system has lcm supported on at most four odd primes, for any exponents (Theorem 3); three primes by a short argument (Theorem 4); five primes conditionally on a "CRT coverage maximality" conjecture, unconditionally for 98.2 % of exponent configurations.

## Method
"Weight function method": partition moduli into prime-power towers and composites; the towers cover at most $\sigma_p = \sum_{j\le a} p^{-j} \le 1/(p-1)$ of $\mathbb{Z}/p^{a}$; the weight region $W = \{n : n \bmod p^{a_p} \text{ uncovered by the } p\text{-tower for all } p\}$ has $|W| = L \prod (p-2)/(p-1)$; a composite $d$ covers at most $\frac{L}{d}\prod_{p \nmid d}\frac{p-2}{p-1}$ of $W$ (stated for $p\mid d$ factors as $1$); union bound gives ratio $R = 41/45 < 1$ for $\{3,5,7,11\}$ in the worst case. Bonferroni corrections for five primes. No formalization.
