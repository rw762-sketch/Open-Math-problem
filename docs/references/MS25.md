# McNew–Setty: covering numbers up to 10^6

*Summary [MS25] · arXiv 2507.23041, 2025.*

**TL;DR.** Classifies covering numbers (integers that are the lcm of a distinct covering) to $10^6$ with a MIP solver; their inequality (eq. (10), Lemma 4.10) is a pairwise-coprime inclusion–exclusion necessary condition. On odd non-deficient numbers it first fails at $11\,486\,475 = 3^3 5^2 7\cdot11\cdot13\cdot17$ (our computation). No odd bound is stated and nothing is machine-checked. We brute-force validated the lemma on 300 random small instances (0 violations).
