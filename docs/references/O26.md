# O26 — Öztürk, "Closed-form nonexistence certificates for odd covering systems" (preprint, Aug 2026)

*Source: ResearchGate publication 412442828, announced on the erdosproblems.com #7 forum (18 Aug 2026). Read from the forum post only; the preprint itself was not checked.*

- Defines $u(M)$ = minimum number of uncovered residues mod $M$ when one class is chosen for each divisor $d \mid M$, $d > 1$; an odd covering with lcm $M$ exists iff $u(M) = 0$.
- Certificates: a "peel" threshold $\sigma(M/p^k)/(M/p^k) < 2 - (1-p^{-k})/(p-1)$, and two volume bounds on the CRT product grid of survivors of the prime-power classes (the same grid as our tower certificate's $U$). Exact rational arithmetic, no search.
- Claims: no odd distinct covering has lcm $< 11\,486\,475$; $u(3^a5^b7^c) \ge 1$ for all exponents; $u(qr) = qr - q - r$ for distinct odd primes; a conjectured three-prime formula.
- Relation to this repo: the tower certificate and his volume bound appear to be the same first-order idea; both stop at $11\,486\,475$. Our distorted certificate goes past it to $1\,527\,701\,175$, kernel-checked in Lean. Not formalized there.
