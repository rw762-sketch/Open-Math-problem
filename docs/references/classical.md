# Classical results used for context

- **Berger–Felzenbaum–Fraenkel (1986–88).** Necessary condition for a distinct covering with lcm $N$: $\prod_{p\mid N}\frac{p-1}{p-2} - \sum_{p\mid N}\frac{1}{p-2} > 2$ (as restated in [S26]). Implies $\omega(N) \ge 5$ for odd $N$.
- **Simpson–Zeilberger, Acta Arith. 59 (1991).** Prime replacement principle (a covering on primes $q_i$ pulls back to the smaller primes $p_i \le q_i$); sieve lower bound on uncovered integers for squarefree moduli; odd squarefree lcm needs $\ge 18$ primes.
- **Guo–Sun (2005).** Odd squarefree lcm needs $\ge 22$ primes.
- **Hough (2015).** Minimum modulus of a distinct covering is $\le 10^{16}$ (later $616\,000$ by BBMST).
- **Hough–Nielsen (2019).** Every distinct covering has a modulus divisible by 2 or 3.
- **Balister–Bollobás–Morris–Sahasrabudhe–Tiba (2022).** No odd distinct covering with squarefree moduli; distorted-sieve method.
- **Filaseta–Kalogirou (2024).** Reciprocal sum bounded away from 1 when the minimum modulus exceeds 4.
